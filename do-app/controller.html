Data Deserts

Map controller

Data Deserts shows how limited investment in data leaves little overlapping evidence across Ecology, Hydrology, Agriculture, and Public Health. Without comparable coverage, cross-domain research is constrained where joined-up evidence is most needed.

This visualisation focuses on countries covered by the Demographic and Health Surveys (DHS) Program. This is a survey-coverage scope rather than an income classification.

Selections are broadcast live to every connected renderer.
