Data Deserts

Datasets behind the map

Eight sources describe where ecological, agricultural, and public-health evidence exists. Counts measure data coverage—not comparable quantities of species, animals, farms, or people.

BioTIMEEcology

BioTIME database of biodiversity time series

Provider
BioTIME Project, University of St Andrews
Years with records
1903–2023
Description
Biodiversity observation records from assemblage time series, assigned to countries from their coordinates. Counts indicate record coverage, not abundance or species richness.
Licence
Individual studies retain their own licences. Users should consult study metadata and cite both BioTIME and the original study.
Source
biotime.st-andrews.ac.uk
LPDEcology

Living Planet Database

Provider
Zoological Society of London and WWF
Years with records
1950–2020
Description
Yearly observations from monitored vertebrate population time series. Counts are population observations, not numbers of animals or Living Planet Index values.
Licence
Living Planet Database Data Use Policy. Access requires contact details, intended use, and acceptance of attribution and sharing conditions.
Source
Living Planet data portal
PREDICTSEcology

Projecting Responses of Ecological Diversity In Changing Terrestrial Systems

Provider
Natural History Museum, London
Years with records
1984–2013
Description
Terrestrial biodiversity sampling sites assigned to the midpoint year of sampling. Sites—not species-level measurements—are counted to avoid inflating intensively sampled locations.
Licence
Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0).
Source
PREDICTS database V1.1
GBIFEcology

Global Biodiversity Information Facility

Provider
GBIF network and contributing data publishers
Years with records
1900–2026
Description
Filtered modern biodiversity occurrence records with coordinates, no flagged geospatial issue, and present status. Fossil and living-specimen records are excluded.
Licence
Record-level source datasets use CC0, CC BY 4.0, or CC BY-NC 4.0. Applicable attribution and non-commercial terms must be followed.
Source
GBIF occurrence search
LSMS-ISAAgriculture

Living Standards Measurement Study — Integrated Surveys on Agriculture

Provider
World Bank
Years with records
2008–2024
Description
Rows in classified agricultural modules covering crops, inputs, plots, land, farms, livestock, fisheries, and trees. Counts are records, not unique farmers, farms, plots, or animals.
Licence
World Bank Microdata Library study-level access types, licences, and citation requirements apply.
Source
LSMS-ISA programme
DHSPublic Health

Demographic and Health Surveys

Provider
The DHS Program, implemented by ICF
Years with records
1985–2025
Description
Interviewed women plus interviewed men reported for each survey. Household totals are not counted as people. DHS coverage defines the countries displayed by Data Deserts.
Licence
Access is project-specific under DHS terms of use. DHS microdata must not be redistributed or exposed through a data tool.
Source
DHS Program API
MICSPublic Health

Multiple Indicator Cluster Surveys

Provider
UNICEF
Years with records
1999–2025
Description
Interviewed women plus interviewed men where individual questionnaire files are available. Household rosters and child files are excluded to avoid duplicate counting.
Licence
UNICEF and survey-specific access, use, acknowledgement, and citation conditions apply.
Source
UNICEF MICS surveys
LSMSPublic Health

Living Standards Measurement Study

Provider
World Bank
Years with records
1985–2024
Description
Person or household-member records from one selected roster per study. Plot, crop, enterprise, expenditure, and transaction files cannot inflate participant coverage.
Licence
World Bank Microdata Library study-level access types, licences, and citation requirements apply.
Source
LSMS collection
Missing country-year records mean missing data, not confirmed zero observations. Always consult the original provider before reuse.
