Data Deserts

An interactive map of how little the datasets from different research domains — ecology, hydrology, agriculture, public health — overlap geographically. The focus is on whether data exists, not its quality.

  1. Each country is shaded by how many of the four domains have data there (red = none, a data desert → green = all four).
  2. Click any country to see exactly which datasets cover it.
  3. In the left panel, switch “Include global-coverage datasets” off to strip away the products that blanket the planet by default — and reveal the real deserts.
