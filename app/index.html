Coverage summary

Counted per country (180 on the map).
Coverage is at country level, derived from the team's Data Sources inventory. Datasets that aim for global coverage span all land; targeted surveys (DHS, MICS, LSMS-ISA) and regional products are mapped to their country lists. The focus is on presence of these specific datasets — not data quality, and not all data that may exist elsewhere.

few / nonemany
Each glow = density of discharge-gauging stations. Zoom in to see gauged vs. ungauged basins — like GBIF, this "global" dataset is really a scatter of local stations.
