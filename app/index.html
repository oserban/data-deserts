Data Deserts

Data Deserts shows how uncoordinated and sector-limited investment in data can leave little overlapping evidence across Ecology, Hydrology, Agriculture, and Public Health—domains that are deeply interconnected. Without comparable coverage across them, cross-domain research is constrained precisely where joined-up evidence is most needed.

This map focuses on countries covered by the Demographic and Health Surveys (DHS) Program. DHS studies mainly cover low- and middle-income countries, though this is a survey-coverage scope rather than an income classification.
