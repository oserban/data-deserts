Data Deserts

This map focuses only on countries covered by the Demographic and Health Surveys (DHS) Program. DHS studies typically focus on low- and middle-income countries (LMICs), although coverage is not identical to an income classification. See the official DHS country list for included countries and the World Bank's current income groups to identify coverage outside the current LMIC grouping.

Overall record assessment

Each country has one four-part chart. Colour strength compares countries within the same domain only; it does not compare biodiversity records with gauges or surveys.

GRDC river gauges (10,701 stations)
few / nonemany
Each glow = density of discharge-gauging stations. Zoom in to see gauged vs. ungauged basins — like GBIF, this "global" dataset is really a scatter of local stations.

Where does data exist — and where doesn't it?

This map gives an overall, domain-segmented record assessment for countries included in the DHS Program. Each segment is normalized only against the same domain in other DHS countries. Pick a time window and click a country to inspect its underlying data. Countries without DHS coverage remain greyed out.
