Data Deserts

Data Deserts shows how limited investment in data leaves little overlapping evidence across Ecology, Hydrology, Agriculture, and Public Health—domains that are deeply interconnected. Without comparable coverage across them, cross-domain research is constrained precisely where joined-up evidence is most needed.

This map focuses on countries covered by the Demographic and Health Surveys (DHS) Program. DHS studies mainly cover low- and middle-income countries, though this is a survey-coverage scope rather than an income classification.

Where does data exist — and where doesn't it?

This map gives an overall, dataset-segmented record assessment for countries included in the DHS Program. Each radial segment is normalized only against the same dataset or category in other DHS-covered countries. Pick a time window, filter the included datasets, and click a country to inspect its underlying records. Countries without DHS participant data remain greyed out.
